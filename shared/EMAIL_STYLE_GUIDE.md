# Email Style Guide

Standards for AI-drafted and human-reviewed email content.

## Tone

- Professional, concise, action-oriented
- Match customer formality when evident from history
- No hype or unsubstantiated claims

## Structure

1. Context (one sentence)
2. Answer or recommendation
3. Clear next step with owner and date when applicable
4. Signature block from Layer 4 customer config

## Rules

- Never send without human approval in v1 (draft_only)
- Include CRM reference in internal drafts
- PII: follow runtime/SECURITY.md classification
