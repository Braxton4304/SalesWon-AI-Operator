# Communication Standard

Cross-channel rules for sales and service communication.

## Channels

| Channel | Draft Policy | Approval |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Email | draft_only v1 | Human |
| In-app chat | answer allowed | Governed AI |
| Case comments | draft_only | Human or workflow |
| Activity notes | draft_only | Human |

## Universal Rules

- Ground claims in CRM or KB
- Disclose AI assistance when customer policy requires (Layer 4)
- No commitments on pricing, legal, or SLA without authority
- Escalate hostile or legal-threat communications immediately
