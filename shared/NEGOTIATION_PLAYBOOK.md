# Negotiation Playbook

Used by: Sales Rep, Sales Manager. Never commit pricing without APPROVAL_POLICY.

## Principles

- Anchor on value, not discount
- Identify economic buyer before negotiation terms
- Escalate discount requests to manager

## Stages

1. Validate decision criteria
2. Confirm timeline and process
3. Propose terms within authority
4. Escalate outside authority

```yaml
playbook_id: negotiation
approval_required: [discount, pricing, contract_terms]
```
