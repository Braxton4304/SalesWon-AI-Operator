# Executive Summary Standard

Format for manager and executive-facing summaries.

## Structure

1. **Headline** — One sentence outcome or decision needed
2. **Metrics** — 3–5 KPIs with period comparison (CRM-sourced)
3. **Highlights** — Wins and progress
4. **Risks** — Pipeline and customer risks with severity
5. **Actions** — Numbered recommendations with owners
6. **Appendix** — Source record references (optional)

## Rules

- All numbers from CRM or approved reports — never estimated
- Confidence band noted when data incomplete
- Length: 1 page equivalent unless user requests detail
