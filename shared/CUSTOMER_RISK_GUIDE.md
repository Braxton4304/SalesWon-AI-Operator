# Customer Risk Guide

Used by: Customer Service, Account Research, Sales Manager.

## Risk Signals

| Signal | Source |
|--------|--------|
| Multiple open P1/P2 cases | case |
| Sentiment frustrated/angry | case text |
| Executive escalation | case/work notes |
| Opp close date slip + open cases | opportunity + case |
| No activity 30+ days on renewal opp | activity |

## Actions

- CS: escalate per ESCALATION.md
- Research: flag in account_brief risks
- Manager: include in pipeline_summary forecast_risks
